## Notes

- Cat6a is worth the minor premium over Cat6 — future 10G to the desktop, better shielding
- Buy the 1000ft box — you'll use it, and per-foot cost is much lower than pre-made cables
- Cable tester is non-negotiable — verify every run before closing walls or patching
- ENT conduit only in exposed basement ceiling; all in-wall runs use no conduit (fish tape approach)
