# Server Closet Spec

## What to Tell Your Contractor

This is the full specification sheet for the server closet framing project.

## Dimensions

- **Interior footprint:** 8' × 6' minimum (larger is better — don't regret going small)
- **Height:** Full basement ceiling height (standard 7'–8' in 1998 construction)
- **Location criteria:**
  - Central basement location (minimizes cable run lengths)
  - Under a continuous interior wall (no plumbing, no bathroom above)
  - Away from water heater, washer/dryer, any water sources
  - Near electrical panel (minimize new circuit run length)

## Framing

- **Studs:** 2×4, 16" on center
- **Blocking:** Extra horizontal blocking at 36" and 48" height (for mounting anything to any wall)
- **Top plate:** Double top plate standard

## Door

- **Width:** 36" minimum (rack equipment moves through this door)
- **Type:** Louvered door — passive ventilation, allows airflow without mechanical fans
- **Swing:** Opens outward (into the room, not into the closet — space is valuable inside)

## Rear Wall (Backer)

- **Material:** 3/4" plywood, floor-to-ceiling, full width
- **Purpose:** Mount anything, anywhere, at any height without needing to hit studs
- **Finish:** Leave raw or paint — not required to be finished

## Floor

- **Treatment:** Sealed or painted concrete (DryLok or concrete floor paint)
- **Purpose:** Moisture barrier — prevents concrete vapor from rising into equipment area
- **Timing:** Seal before framing if possible; seal after framing if not

## Ventilation

- **Primary:** Louvered door provides passive airflow (cool air in at bottom, warm air out near top)
- **Supplemental (if needed):** Wall vent near ceiling on one wall — allows hot air to escape without mechanical fan
- **Active exhaust:** Add a 120mm or 240mm exhaust fan if ambient temperature exceeds 85°F regularly

## Temperature Targets

| Target | Action |
|--------|--------|
| 60–75°F | Ideal operating range |
| 75–85°F | Acceptable, monitor |
| >85°F | Add active exhaust ventilation |

## Fire Safety

- **Smoke detector:** Required inside server closet
- **Fire extinguisher:** ABC dry chemical, mounted near door exterior (grab it on the way in)

## Electrical

See [electrical-plan.md](./electrical-plan.md) for full circuit specifications.

Summary:
- 2× dedicated 20A/120V circuits for equipment
- 1× general purpose circuit for dehumidifier, lights, tools
- 1× capped 240V/30A circuit (future-proofing)
- All GFCI protected (basement requirement)
- Overhead light + switch at door

## Pre-Close Checklist

Before closing drywall, verify:

- [ ] All electrical conduit / wiring roughed in and inspected
- [ ] All low-voltage cable runs completed (network, cameras, etc.)
- [ ] Plywood backer installed
- [ ] Floor sealed
- [ ] Any penetrations (cable, conduit) through walls blocked with firestop putty or foam
- [ ] Smoke detector rough-in location marked
